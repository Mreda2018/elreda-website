What is Elreda Advertising?

Elreda Advertising is an Egyptian company based in Ebshan, Bila district, Kafr El-Sheikh governorate, serving clients in Egypt, the UAE and the Gulf states. It offers four core services: visual identity and print design; professional printing with full finishing; design and development of websites and online stores; and accounting and tax systems compliant with the Egyptian Tax Authority and the UAE Federal Tax Authority. Digital services are delivered fully remotely to any country, while printing is produced in Egypt at our own facility and in the UAE through a local printing partner that prints and delivers directly to the client. Clients outside Egypt

How we work with clients in the UAE and the Gulf

Most of our work is digital, which means distance is not an obstacle. We work with clients in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain to the same standard and the same deadlines — and in the UAE we also handle printing and delivery through a local printing partner, so you get design and print from a single point of contact.

Delivered fully remotely

Brand identity and usage guidelines · social media design · print and packaging design (press-ready files you can print locally) · business websites and online stores · hosting, domains and professional email · accounting and tax systems with team training.

Printing and production

Inside Egypt we print and finish the work ourselves. In the UAE we work with a local printing partner that produces the job and delivers it straight to the client — so design, print and delivery come from one place instead of you sourcing a printer yourself. Elsewhere in the Gulf we hand over press-ready files to the correct specification (CMYK, bleed, proper resolution) and review the proof with you before you approve it.

Communication and scheduling

We work over WhatsApp, email and Zoom or Google Meet, whichever suits you. Cairo and Abu Dhabi are only one hour apart, so our working hours line up almost exactly — and delivery times are the same as those listed in the services table.

Pricing and payment

Digital services for clients outside Egypt are priced in US dollars and shown in the services table; printing in the UAE is quoted on request, because it depends on material, quantity and finishing. Payment by international bank transfer, Wise or PayPal, on the usual terms: 50% up front and 50% on delivery.
