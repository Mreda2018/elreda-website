What's in this portfolio?

The boards below were produced by the Elreda team and cover three kinds of work: visual identity systems (logo variants, color palette and usage rules), printed identity applications (cards, envelopes, invoices, stamps, menus, catalogues, signage and packaging), and digital solutions (business sites, corporate sites, online stores, official email and hosting). Every board opens full size for detail.

Print & Applications

Visual Identity Applications

A complete print application board: business cards, letterheads, envelopes, invoices and vouchers, stamps, flyers and brochures, menus, catalogue, stickers, product labels, packaging, barcode, roll-up, pop-up, backdrop and shop front.

Websites & Stores

The Digital Ecosystem

The digital ecosystem: business website, corporate site, online store with payment gateways (Visa, Mastercard, Meeza, Fawry), mobile storefront, official email, hosting and domain dashboard, and an online order form.

Brand Identity

Visual Identity System

The visual identity system: primary, reversed and monochrome logo plus a pattern derived from the mark, with the official color palette, brand values and the full service list.

Brand Identity

Brand Guide & Color Palette

The brand guide on a single board: logo variants, the color codes #8B1E21, #111111, #F5F5F5 and #6B6B6B, and the three values the brand is built on.

Print & Applications

Extended Identity Applications

Extended identity applications: staff ID, mug, t-shirt, cap, tote and paper bags, vehicle branding, meeting-room wall, notebook, receipt voucher, presentation folder and a PDF invoice.

Websites & Stores

Integrated Digital Solutions

An integrated digital solutions board with realistic mockups of sites and stores across devices, plus identity applications in the real world from shop front to billboard.

Brand Identity

Digital & Environmental Applications

Digital and environmental identity applications: website hero, mobile app, social posts, outdoor sign, reception wall, billboard, wrapped van, and a set of printed items and gifts.
