Our Story

We started with one idea: small businesses deserve big-company quality

At Elreda Advertising we believe every business — whatever its size — deserves a respectable visual identity, high-quality print materials and a digital presence that does it justice. So we brought design, printing, development and accounting systems into one place, so you never have to deal with five different suppliers for one project.

We work with a marketing mindset first: we ask about your business goal before opening any design software, and build every visual decision on a clear rationale. The result isn't just a beautiful design — it's a tool that sells and a brand you can build on.

What is Elreda Advertising?

Elreda Advertising is an Egyptian company based in Ebshan, Bila district, Kafr El-Sheikh governorate, offering four core services under one roof: visual identity and print design; printing of all kinds with professional finishing; design and development of websites and online stores; and the supply and setup of accounting and tax systems integrated with the e-invoice and e-receipt platforms of the Egyptian Tax Authority. The company serves clients in Kafr El-Sheikh and across every Egyptian governorate. Contact via WhatsApp at +201050020785 or email info@elreda-ads.com.
