The 30-second summary

  • Your company needs a software solution when repetitive manual work (copying and pasting, multiple Excel sheets, follow-up messages) takes more time than your team has.
  • You have 3 options: off-the-shelf software, integration and automation between the tools you already use, or a custom system. Always start with the simplest one that solves the problem.
  • AI is genuinely useful for: answering repeated customer questions, extracting data from invoices and documents, and summarising data and reports.
  • Every successful software project starts by mapping the current process and measuring the time wasted in it, not by picking a technology.

How do you know your company needs a software solution?

If most of these signs show up in your company, you are paying for manual work every day:

  • The same data is typed into more than one place: the sales sheet, the accounts, the shipping tool.
  • The weekly or monthly report takes someone hours to put together by hand.
  • Nobody knows a customer's order status without asking on WhatsApp.
  • Customer service answers the same questions dozens of times a day.
  • Prices or stock levels are often wrong because of manual copying.
  • How the work is done lives in one employee's head, and their absence stops it.
  • You cannot quickly answer: which product makes the most profit? Which customer pays latest?

The rule: do not build from scratch what already exists in a good form. Accounting and e-invoicing, for example, have ready-made systems such as Faktra for Egypt and the UAE. Custom development is for the part that makes your way of working different.

Tasks that get automated in most companies

  1. Answering repeated questions

    A chatbot on the website or WhatsApp answers questions about prices, opening hours, location and order status, and hands over to a person only what needs one.

  2. Entering invoice and document data

    Instead of typing supplier invoices by hand, the system reads the invoice from a photo or PDF and extracts the supplier, items and amounts, and an employee reviews them before saving.

  3. Recurring reports

    Sales, expenses, stock and overdue payments are gathered automatically into a report or dashboard that reaches you every morning or every week.

  4. Order and payment follow-up

    An automatic message to the customer at each stage of the order, a reminder before an invoice falls due, and an alert to your team about any delay.

  5. Connecting your tools

    The online store, the accounting system, the courier and Google Sheets exchange data through APIs, with no copying and pasting.

Where AI really helps, and where it does not

Genuinely usefulNeeds caution
Understanding customer questions phrased in different ways and answering from your company's informationFinancial or legal decisions without human review
Extracting data from invoices, contracts and imagesAny task that needs 100% accuracy without review
Summarising reports, correspondence and meetingsPutting sensitive customer data into public tools without safeguards
Drafting replies and content for an employee to reviewReplacing your team's expertise entirely

The most successful uses let AI prepare and a person decide: it extracts the data and suggests the reply, and the employee reviews and approves in seconds instead of minutes.

How to start a software project without it getting out of hand

  1. Map the current process

    Where does it start? Who does what? Where is the data written down? Pen and paper are enough.

  2. Measure the time wasted

    How many hours a week does this process take? How many errors happen a month? That number is what justifies the project.

  3. Start with the smallest useful version

    Pick the most frequent problem and solve it first, and try it with your team for two weeks before expanding.

  4. Make sure you own the code and the data

    Agree in writing that the code and the database belong to you, along with how backups and user permissions work.

  5. Plan for maintenance

    Every system needs monitoring and updates. Know from the start who will look after it, and at what cost.

What determines the cost of a software solution?

  • The number of processes and screens: automating one report is different from a full operations system.
  • The number of systems to integrate: each connection to an external tool takes development and testing time.
  • The number of users and permissions: a system for one person is simpler than one for a team with different roles.
  • The quality of your current data: cleaning old, scattered data can take longer than the development itself.
  • AI: adds running costs that depend on usage.

That is why there is no serious fixed price for any "system", and any quote given before your process is understood is a guess. The right way is a session to understand the process, then a written proposal with the scope, timeline and cost.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between automation and AI?

Automation carries out defined steps automatically (if this happens, do that). AI handles unstructured input, such as understanding a question written in any form or reading a photographed invoice. Most successful solutions combine the two.

Do small companies benefit from software solutions?

Yes, often more than large ones, because every hour saved for the owner or a single employee makes a direct difference. Start by automating one repetitive task before any large system.

Can WhatsApp be connected to my system?

Yes, through the official WhatsApp Business API, to send order confirmations and reminders, or to run a chatbot that answers customers and hands over to a person when needed.

Is my company's data safe with AI?

It depends on how it is built. A good solution decides which data is sent to the model and which is not, uses services with clear terms that your data is not used for training, and keeps permissions and a log of every operation.

Do I need a custom accounting system?

Usually not. Accounting and e-invoicing have ready-made, tax-compliant systems such as Faktra for Egypt and the UAE. It is better to connect the ready-made system to the rest of your business than to build accounting from scratch.
